Our Origin

Fifteen Years Of Slow, Thoughtful Travel

Founded on the sun-drenched shores of North Goa in 2011, WanderNest was born from a simple conviction: holidays should be felt deeply, not rushed through.

How We Began

Bespoke Journeys, Handcrafted With Soul

In 2011, we were exhausted by commercial tour buses packing 45 travelers onto rushed schedules. We believed that true luxury lies in privacy, silence, authentic regional flavors, and the unhurried liberty to linger wherever your heart pauses.

Over fifteen years, our team of dedicated regional specialists has forged personal friendships with heritage palace owners in Rajasthan, Shikara captains on Dal Lake, and plantation masters in Coorg. We craft every itinerary like an author crafts a novel.

Our Philosophy

The WanderNest Principles

Four bedrock values that guide every route, hotel partnership, and private experience we curate.

01 / SLOW LUXURY

Unrushed Pacing

No waking up at 5 AM to catch commercial group transfers. Your day unfolds around your natural circadian rhythm.

02 / PRIVACY

Private Chaperones

Private air-conditioned SUVs, dedicated chauffeurs, and hand-selected local historians at every archaeological site.

03 / SUSTAINABILITY

Community Pledge

We work exclusively with boutique indigenous stays and fair-wage village artisans, leaving zero carbon footprints behind.

04 / ASSURANCE

24/7 Concierge

Direct WhatsApp access to your trip specialist around the clock — ensuring instant medical, luggage, or itinerary flexibility.
