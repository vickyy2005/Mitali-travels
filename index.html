Signature Wanderlust

Six Ways To Rediscover India

Private guides, untouched detours, and access to places you cannot simply book through an online travel app.

Private Waterway

Alleppey Luxury Kettuvallam Living

Gliding through mirror-like canal backwaters on a private carved cedar barge with personal chef and butler catering local coconut curry delicacies.

High Altitude

Stargazing Glamping at Pangong Tso

Sleep in insulated geodome tents at 14,270 ft. Warm yourself by a cedar wood fire while the entire galaxy unfolds in crystal clear unpolluted Himalayan skies.

Oceanic Safari

PADI Scuba & Coral Garden Walks

Dive into pristine transparent waters off Havelock and Neil islands alongside sea turtles, manta rays, and thriving kaleidoscopic coral reefs.

Royal Heritage

Royal Haveli Stays & Sunset Cruises

Live like Rajput royalty in converted 18th-century lake palaces with private terrace dining, vintage car transfers, and bespoke gemstone shopping tours.

Slow Living

Coorg & Munnar Tea Estate Havens

Wake up to morning birdsong and mountain mist. Hand-pick single origin Arabica coffee cherries, taste rare orthodox tea brews, and unwind in antique colonial villas.

Desert Miracle

White Desert Moonrise & Rogan Art

Witness the ethereal spectacle of the Great Rann gleaming like liquid silver at moonrise. Meet 8th-generation masters of rare Rogan fabric art in Nirona village.
